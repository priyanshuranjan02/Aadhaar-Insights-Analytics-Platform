# Data Quality Audit and Forecasting Sensitivity Analysis

## 1. Objective

This analysis investigates unusual daily activity totals and potential reporting-coverage issues in the Aadhaar enrolment, demographic, and biometric datasets. It also evaluates whether excluding two exceptionally low-coverage biometric observations affects forecasting performance.

## 2. Data Quality Audit

The audit parses dates, identifies exact duplicate records, aggregates activity by observed date, and calculates signed robust scores for daily row counts and activity totals.

| Dataset | Observed dates | Exact duplicate rows removed | Unusual activity dates flagged | Low-coverage dates flagged |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Enrolment | 92 | 22,957 | 4 | 15 |
| Demographic | 95 | 473,601 | 5 | 5 |
| Biometric | 89 | 94,896 | 5 | 11 |

The low-coverage counts were obtained after correcting the robust-score calculation to preserve the direction of deviations from the median.

## 3. Biometric Low-Coverage Observations

Two observations were particularly unusual:

| Date | Raw rows | Activity total | Row-count robust score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2025-12-22 | 36 | 39 | -11.911 |
| 2025-12-23 | 280 | 289 | -11.778 |

Nearby dates contained substantially more records. These observations are consistent with a potential reporting or extraction-coverage problem, although the underlying cause cannot be established from the available aggregate data.

The raw data and primary cleaned dataset were not modified to remove these observations.

## 4. Matched-Origin Forecasting Experiment

Four scenarios were evaluated on the same 58 target dates, with a minimum history requirement of 24 observations:

| Scenario | Model | MAE | RMSE | R² |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A — Original history | Linear Regression | 97,847.40 | 129,639.96 | -0.0836 |
| B — Exclusion history | Linear Regression | 95,647.31 | 124,140.80 | 0.0063 |
| C — Original history | Persistence | 112,755.60 | 158,667.64 | -0.6232 |
| D — Exclusion history | Persistence | 109,582.26 | 150,185.35 | -0.4543 |

For linear regression, excluding the two suspect dates from the available history reduced MAE by approximately 2.25% and RMSE by approximately 4.24%.

## 5. Interpretation and Limitations

The sensitivity experiment indicates a modest improvement when the two low-coverage observations are excluded from the historical inputs. However, the near-zero R² in the exclusion scenario indicates limited predictive performance.

The results do not establish why the observations are unusually small, prove that exclusion is universally appropriate, or demonstrate that the model generalizes well to future dates. The experiment should therefore remain a sensitivity analysis rather than a justification for permanently deleting observations.

## 6. Data Handling Decision

- Preserve the original raw datasets.
- Preserve the primary cleaned daily series.
- Retain the audit output for traceability.
- Keep the exclusion experiment separate from the primary forecasting pipeline.
- Reconsider any production change only after further validation and evaluation.

## 7. Reproducibility

The audit is generated by `src/forecasting_outlier_audit.py`.

The matched-origin sensitivity experiment is generated by `src/forecasting_matched_origin.py`.

The audit output is saved to `data/processed/forecasting_clean/daily_quality_audit.csv`. The sensitivity experiment outputs are saved under `data/processed/forecasting_v2/sensitivity/matched_origin/`.
